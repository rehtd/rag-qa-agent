q_zh: 香港银行一般会收取哪些费用？
a_zh: 银行常见收费包括账户月费、转账和汇款手续费、自动柜员机（ATM）跨行提款费，以及某些柜台服务费。具体项目和各行的收费水平并不相同，开户或办理服务前应向银行索取收费表，以各银行公布为准。
q_en: What fees do Hong Kong banks typically charge?
a_en: Common bank charges include monthly account fees, transfer and remittance fees, interbank ATM withdrawal fees, and some counter service fees. The exact items and amounts vary by bank, so you should ask for the bank's fee schedule before opening an account or using a service.

q_zh: 国际汇款的手续费会很高吗？
a_zh: 国际汇率交易一般涉及手续费、外汇差价，收款银行也可能另外扣取费用，因此总成本可能高于本地转账。办理前应了解所有相关费用及到账币种金额，并按需要比较不同银行或汇款渠道，没有统一标准，以各银行为准。
q_en: Are international remittance fees high?
a_en: International remittances usually involve handling fees, the foreign-exchange spread, and possibly charges deducted by the receiving bank, so the total cost can be higher than a local transfer. Understand all the charges and the amount actually received before you proceed, and compare banks or channels as needed — there is no single standard, as fees vary by bank.

q_zh: 存款保障计划最多保障多少钱？
a_zh: 香港的存款保障计划为每名存户在每间银行提供最高 80 万港元的保障。若银行倒闭，你在这间银行的合资格存款会获得赔偿，最高为 80 万港元。
q_en: How much does the Deposit Protection Scheme cover?
a_en: Hong Kong's Deposit Protection Scheme protects up to HK$800,000 per depositor per bank. If a bank fails, you will be compensated for your eligible deposits with that bank, up to a maximum of HK$800,000.

q_zh: 信用卡只还最低还款额有什么风险？
a_zh: 只偿还最低还款额虽然能避免逾期，但未偿还的结欠会持续累积利息，令债务越滚越大，最终还款总额可能远高于原来的消费金额。最好尽量清还全部结欠，避免被高息拖累。
q_en: What are the risks of paying only the minimum credit card amount?
a_en: Paying only the minimum amount avoids being in default, but the outstanding balance keeps accruing interest, which can grow the debt over time and make the total repayment much larger than the original spending. It is best to pay off the balance in full to avoid being dragged down by high interest.
source: https://www.hkma.gov.hk/chi/smart-consumers/credit-cards/
